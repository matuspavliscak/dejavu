05 / Keep it consistent

A few simple rules.

  1. Give it room.Clear space: at least one quarter of the symbol's height around the artwork.
  2. Keep it readable.Full logo: 160px minimum width. Symbol: 24px minimum in general UI.
  3. Keep the accents.Always write Déjà Vu, with both accents. Use the supplied wordmark.
  4. Let the shape work.Preserve proportions. Keep the artwork flat and the background quiet.
